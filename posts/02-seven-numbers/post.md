**Thread:**

> 1/ Show rate = calls held (CRM) ÷ calls booked (calendar).
> Match per person — otherwise you're dividing two different lists.
>
> 2/ Speed to lead = first call − form sent, paired per lead.
> Report the median. One lead left for three days shouldn't hide ninety fast ones.
>
> 3/ Cost per held meeting = spend ÷ meetings that actually happened.
> Booked is the number that makes the report look good.
>
> 4/ Reply-to-meeting rate = meetings booked ÷ replies.
> The only number that tells you if your copy works.
>
> 5/ Revenue per lead = cash collected ÷ leads.
> What a lead is worth, so you know what you can pay for one.
>
> 6/ Revenue per subscriber = launch revenue ÷ list size.
> Compare launches with the same rules every time.
>
> 7/ Revenue from your list = orders from people who are on your list, refunds out.
> Matching, not attribution.
>
> 8/ We built Namzilabs to make all 7 without SQL — each one shows its working: namzilabs.co (free, no card)
