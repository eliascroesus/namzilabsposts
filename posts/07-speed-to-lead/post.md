> 1/ Your speed to lead is probably wrong.
>
> Not because your team is slow — because of how it's measured. Three common mistakes:
>
> 2/ Averaging it.
> One lead left for three days hides ninety you called in five minutes. The average says "4 hours". The truth is "fast, with one disaster". Report the median, and show the disaster separately.
>
> 3/ Letting uncalled leads vanish.
> A lead nobody called has no "time to call", so most reports quietly drop it. It's the slowest lead of all. Count it.
>
> 4/ Matching form and call by time alone.
> The form is in Typeform, the call is in Aircall. Pair them by the *same person* — email or phone — or you'll pair Dana's form with Marco's call.
>
> 5/ Done right: earliest form → first call, per person, median across leads, and a receipt with how many were never called.
