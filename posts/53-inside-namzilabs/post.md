## Alt text

- **ig-01:** "This is Namzilabs. From the inside." Real screens, swipe for the tour. A real screenshot of the dashboard's Overview tab: leads, booked leads and calls showed; booking rate and show-up rate; revenue by week as a bar chart.
- **ig-02:** "See exactly where your funnel breaks." Every source gets its own funnel. Real screenshots of two source funnels (Instagram and Facebook), each from leads to booked leads, call shows and customers, with the rate between each step.
- **ig-03:** "Build any KPI in three moves." Get the records, narrow them down, turn them into a number. A real screenshot of the flow builder's "Build a metric in three moves" card.
- **ig-04:** "Every step you need." Match, filter, split, total, no code. A real screenshot of the flow builder's step list: Get data, Combine, Match, Filter, Split, Summarize and Break down, each with a one-line description.
- **ig-05:** "Your best day, at a glance." Revenue by day, shaded against the best one. A real screenshot of the revenue calendar: each day's revenue and number of records, with the best day, the average day and the days with data.
- **ig-06:** "Plug in your tools." Each app says what it reads, and how often it syncs. A real screenshot of the Apps page: Calendly, Close CRM, Whop and Google Sheets (connected), each with what it reads and whether it syncs instantly or on a schedule.
- **ig-07:** "Start free." No card. Invite one person and get a month free. All your data in one place; any KPI, no code; it never edits your data.
