Bios & copy.

Every bio, tagline and company description, written to say what Namzilabs does, why it matters and the offer. Each one fits its platform's limit: tap Copy and paste.

How we write: the rules every line follows

    Posts

    Instagram carousels and X images, each with its captions, alt text and a claims check. Tap one to see every slide and copy the copy.

    Videos

    60fps, rendered frame by frame. Silent by design: add a trending sound in the app.

    Logo

    One mark: two tools as two rings, overlapping, with the space between them left empty. It comes in four colourways and the eclipse alternate, plus variants that change the rings' finish and the ground behind them. Every banner design has one made for it.

    Transparent logo files

    For your website, decks and docs: the logo with nothing behind it, in white, black and blue. Cropped tight to the artwork. Use the SVG where you can; the PNGs are for places that won't take one.

    Variants

    Banners by platform.

    Pick where it's going. Every design is shown at that platform's real shape, with the profile picture made for it roughly where your photo or logo sits.

    Banners

    Every design at every size: X, LinkedIn (company and profile), Facebook (page and group), YouTube, the link-preview card and an email signature, each with the profile picture made to go with it. Plus Instagram highlight covers.

    LinkedIn: which file goes where. linkedin-company is the cover of a company page (the page logo sits over its bottom-left). linkedin-profile is the background of your own profile (your photo sits over its bottom-left). Every word stays clear of both, and inside what phones show. Don't upload the X header to LinkedIn: it's a different shape, and LinkedIn crops it under your photo. Tap "See it on LinkedIn" on any design.

    Made for LinkedIn

    Designed for the narrow LinkedIn covers first. Team is for the people who work at Namzilabs, on their own profiles.

    Games & films

    Famous games, films and series, played by Namzi and the rings: the format and the joke, never the characters. Each has its own game-style logo.

    Memes

    For launch weeks and a playful profile. They skip the link preview and email sizes, which stay on-brand.

    Instagram highlight covers

    Meet Namzi.

    The space between the logo's two rings, with a face. For social only: stickers for Stories and replies, and avatars for profile pictures.
